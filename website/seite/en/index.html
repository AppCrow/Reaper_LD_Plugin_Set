LD Tools

Markers for your light show, right in REAPER.

LD Tools are four free tools for lighting designers in REAPER, in one window: set markers from MIDI notes, rename markers for the grandMA3 import, copy groups of markers and measure the tempo live.

Download for macOSDownload for Windows

Free and open source (MIT). For REAPER 7 on macOS and Windows.

A look inside

What you get

One window for everything

Three tabs for renaming, MIDI notes and copying, with the tempo in the header. The window sits docked at the bottom of REAPER and opens with REAPER.

Live tempo

Reads the tempo of an audio item to two decimals, even while REAPER is playing. “Half” and “Double” fix the octave, and “Set project tempo” applies it without moving your items.

Markers from MIDI notes

Every note becomes a marker, one lane per track. Tracks with the same name before the brackets, such as “Kick (12)” and “Kick (13)”, share a lane; existing markers in it are replaced if you like.

Renaming for grandMA3

Builds names in the format Cue(number)[Command]^Sequence^ for a whole selection at once, with a preview. Cue numbers count in the order you click.

Copy markers

A group of markers with their names, colors and spacing, copied to another spot, say for the chorus that comes back.

Installs inside REAPER

A script in REAPER puts everything in place: scripts, actions and the two extensions needed. It suggests shortcuts; you set them yourself.

Frequently asked

What do I need?

REAPER 7, and REAPER 7.72 or later for marker lanes. The extensions ReaImGui and js_ReaScriptAPI come with it, and the installer puts them in place. You do not need SWS.

Why no regular installer?

Only a script inside REAPER can create actions and open the shortcut dialog. That is why you install LD Tools from within REAPER. After the extensions are installed, restart REAPER once.

What is MA-Tools syntax?

A marker name like Kick(1)[Top]^Kick^: cue name, cue number, command and sequence. It is the format the grandMA3 marker import reads.

My shortcut clears the selection. Why?

When the Region/Marker Manager has focus, it swallows the shortcut. Click into the arrange view first, then press the shortcut.

How accurate is the tempo?

In my test with a real song, it was off by less than 0.02 BPM.

How do I get a new version?

Download the new version and run the installer again. There is no automatic notice of new versions.

Privacy in short

The LD Tools code does not access the internet. There is no account, no usage statistics and no crash reports. Everything is stored only on your computer: the scripts, the actions, the two extensions and a few settings such as the last open tab and the last color used in your REAPER folder, and the markers in your project.

The bundled extensions ReaImGui and js_ReaScriptAPI are unmodified builds by their authors; I have not checked their network behavior myself. The download comes from GitHub, where GitHub’s privacy policy applies.
